# Product brief

## Working name

**Kotaru**. Brand capitalization is exactly `Kotaru` unless the owner approves a change. The name requires trademark, domain and App Store clearance before launch.

## Vision

Make a believable animated companion that users can actually afford to talk with for meaningful periods, especially by voice. The experience should feel alive through continuity, fast turn-taking, expressive animation and remembered details—not by pretending the AI is human.

## Initial audience

- Adults 18+ who enjoy conversational AI and animated characters.
- Spanish-speaking users underserved by voice-first companion products.
- English-speaking users seeking better value and longer conversations.
- Users who want casual companionship, roleplay within policy, language practice, reflection and entertainment.

## Launch markets

- Primary commercial market: United States, including English- and Spanish-speaking adults.
- International readiness: localization, currency display, provider routing and privacy configuration must not assume US-only operation.
- Do not launch globally all at once. Recommend the next 2–3 countries only after comparing acquisition cost, provider availability, taxes, app-store requirements and privacy obligations.

The MVP is not for children, medical treatment, mental-health diagnosis or emergency support.

## Jobs to be done

- Talk naturally while walking, cooking or relaxing.
- Continue a relationship across sessions without repeating basic context.
- Choose a personality and visual style that feels comfortable.
- Receive supportive, entertaining and coherent responses.
- Understand exactly how many voice minutes remain and what is remembered.

## Differentiation

1. More usable voice minutes at each paid tier.
2. Spanish-first quality alongside English.
3. Transparent memory center.
4. Fast interruption and natural streaming.
5. Provider routing that protects margins and availability.
6. Honest AI identity and non-manipulative engagement.
7. A polished, modern manga identity with original characters designed for animation and emotional readability on mobile screens.

## MVP scope

- iOS and Android application.
- Email/Apple/Google authentication.
- Three original companion archetypes with selectable voice/personality.
- Text chat and push-to-talk voice.
- Streaming partial responses and barge-in/cancel.
- Basic 3D avatar with visemes, blink, idle, gaze and 6–8 emotion states.
- Short-term session context plus explicit long-term memories.
- Memory center: view, edit, pin and delete.
- Subscription and usage screen.
- English and Spanish UI/conversation.
- Safety reporting, block/reset companion, delete/export account data.
- Analytics limited to product and operational events.
- Modern dark/light design system with manga character cards, restrained motion and premium typography.

## Explicitly out of MVP

- Photorealistic human avatars.
- User-to-user social network.
- Companion marketplace.
- NSFW/sexual content.
- Minors mode.
- Autonomous phone calls or messaging third parties.
- Full duplex always-on background listening.
- Proprietary motion-generation model.
- Claims of therapy or clinical benefit.

## Candidate original manga archetypes

Names are placeholders pending brand clearance:

- **Nova**: curious, energetic, humorous creative companion.
- **Sage**: calm, thoughtful companion for reflection and learning.
- **Rio**: warm, social, bilingual companion focused on everyday conversation.

Each has a structured persona card, boundaries and conversation style. The user may tune warmth, humor, verbosity and initiative without overriding safety.

Character art must be created from original briefs. Avoid prompts such as “in the style of” a named artist, anime, manga, studio or franchise.

## North-star and guardrail metrics

North-star: weekly users who complete at least three meaningful sessions on two or more days.

Supporting metrics:

- activation: first completed voice turn;
- D1/D7/D30 retention;
- median voice minutes per active day;
- speech-end-to-first-audio latency;
- successful interruption rate;
- memory approval/edit/delete rates;
- free-to-paid and paid-tier mix;
- gross margin by plan and heavy-user percentile;
- refund, safety report and deletion rates.

Never optimize session duration using emotional coercion.
