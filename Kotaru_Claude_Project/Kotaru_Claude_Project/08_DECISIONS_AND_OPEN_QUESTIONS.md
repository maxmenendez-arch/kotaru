# Decisions and open questions

## Current assumed decisions

- Product: consumer mobile AI companion.
- Confirmed working name: Kotaru.
- Audience: adults 18+.
- Launch languages: Spanish and English.
- Market: USA first, internationally ready.
- Visual direction: modern premium interface.
- Character direction: original manga characters.
- Initial operating budget ceiling: USD 1,000/month, including 20% contingency.
- Core interaction: text and push-to-talk voice.
- Visual: original stylized 3D avatars.
- Business model: freemium subscription with included voice hours and optional add-ons.
- AI strategy: modular multiprovider routing; Chinese/international economical providers where legally and technically suitable.
- Memory: explicit, transparent and user-editable.
- Content: no NSFW in MVP.
- Platforms: iOS and Android.

## Decisions requiring owner input

2. Desired launch window?
3. Will the company contract providers through a US, Panama, Spain or other entity?
4. Is voice cloning strategically required, or can it remain post-MVP?
5. Is the initial goal a coded product, an investor prototype, or both?
6. Should the manga cast initially be human-only or include fantasy/creature companions?

## Research tasks before provider selection

- Verify current official pricing for Qwen/Alibaba Cloud Model Studio, MiniMax and Volcengine/Doubao in the intended region.
- Confirm account eligibility, payment methods and quotas for the contracting entity.
- Benchmark Spanish accents relevant to Cuba, Puerto Rico, wider Latin America and Spain.
- Review commercial rights and consent requirements for each TTS/voice feature.
- Measure true billed cost, not list-price arithmetic alone.
- Compare against at least one non-Chinese fallback.
- Validate data retention/training configuration in contract and console.
