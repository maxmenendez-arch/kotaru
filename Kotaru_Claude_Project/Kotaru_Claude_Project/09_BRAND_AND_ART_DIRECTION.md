# Kotaru — brand and art direction

## Brand idea

**Kotaru** should feel like a doorway to an expressive character who knows the user over time. The brand is modern, emotionally warm and technologically credible—not childish, clinical or cyberpunk-heavy.

## Positioning line

**A companion with time for you.**

Alternative working lines for testing:

- Talk longer. Feel remembered.
- Your story continues here.
- A voice that remembers.

Do not use a final tagline before trademark and marketing review.

## Visual language

- Modern manga character design with original silhouettes and wardrobes.
- Clean mobile interface with the character occupying the emotional center.
- Deep neutral backgrounds, luminous accent colors and subtle glass/translucent surfaces.
- Rounded but not childish components.
- High-quality portrait framing for conversation; full-body view for onboarding and customization.
- Motion should be soft, responsive and economical on battery.

## Character system

Every character needs:

- original front/side/three-quarter sheets;
- silhouette test at small mobile size;
- facial expression sheet;
- viseme/mouth-shape sheet;
- 8 emotional states;
- 10–15 reusable gestures;
- day/evening outfit variants only after MVP validation;
- written provenance and commercial rights for every asset.

## Initial cast

### Nova — creative spark

- Visual cue: angular bob, asymmetrical accent, violet/pink energy.
- Personality: curious, playful, imaginative.
- Avoid: manic energy, childish speech or copyrighted magical-girl cues.

### Sage — calm clarity

- Visual cue: composed silhouette, soft layers, indigo/aqua palette.
- Personality: thoughtful, patient, concise.
- Avoid: presenting as a therapist, guru or medical professional.

### Rio — social warmth

- Visual cue: relaxed modern streetwear, warm accent color, expressive gestures.
- Personality: bilingual, friendly, everyday conversationalist.
- Avoid: national stereotypes or caricatured Spanish/Latino identity.

Names and appearances remain provisional until clearance and user testing.

## UI signature moments

- Character wakes subtly when a session opens.
- Listening state uses a calm aura/ring, not a surveillance-style waveform.
- Memories appear as editable “moments,” never as hidden dossiers.
- Usage meter shows hours/minutes clearly and offers an economical mode before a hard limit.

## Asset strategy under the bootstrap budget

- Prototype with simple original concept art and one production-ready rig.
- Build one excellent character before commissioning all three in full 3D.
- Use commercially licensed base tooling only when its license permits app distribution and derivative customization.
- Keep authoring files, licenses and source provenance organized.
- Do not train or fine-tune on scraped manga/anime art.

## Brand clearance checklist

- Search trademark conflicts for `Kotaru` and close variants.
- Check domains, App Store and Google Play names, social handles and pronunciation.
- Confirm whether the capitalization is legible and consistently interpreted as “pal + AI.”
- Prepare at least two backup names before spending heavily on production art or acquisition.
